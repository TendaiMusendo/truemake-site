Services

Integration, AI enablement, and the implementation that joins them

Integration makes your systems agree with each other. Enablement puts AI to work on top of them, with support that continues after go-live. Implementation is how both arrive: built by hand and with AI, tested by your team early, judged on whether people use it.

Integration between your systems

Your CRM, ERP, finance system, website and the tools around them share one version of the truth, without retyping.

Record-level syncs between systems and sites, with field mappings written down, edge cases decided up front, and every flow run in a sandbox before it touches production.

  • CRM systems such as HubSpot
  • ERP and finance systems such as NetSuite
  • Integration platforms like Celigo, plus REST APIs and webhooks
  • Websites, forms and workspace tools such as Google Workspace
  • Sandbox-to-production cutover

AI enablement, with support that stays

Your people open Claude because it can see their work and do something about it, and someone is there when it needs adjusting.

Connectors into the systems your team uses every day, skills written for their actual tasks, rules enforced server-side, and hands-on training on their own work. Then ongoing support: a named person, regular check-ins, and changes as use grows.

  • Team onboarding and training
  • Connectors to your CRM, ERP, accounts, calendar and mail
  • Ongoing support and tuning after go-live
  • Usage rules and an audit trail

Implementation and custom software, by hand and with AI

CRM, ERP and software projects where your team is testing from the start, not at the end.

We combine hands-on configuration with Claude Code to get to a first working version early, then iterate with the people who will use it. Internal tools, portals and web apps built for production and handed over with the code.

  • CRM and ERP rollouts, such as HubSpot and NetSuite
  • Custom tools built on React, FastAPI and PostgreSQL
  • Working slice early in the engagement
  • Adoption measured, not assumed

Agents with a person in the loop

The repetitive work is drafted before you sit down. You review and approve.

Scheduled agents that prepare the morning brief, triage the inbox, turn meetings into records, book time and draft invoices. They draft. Your team decides. Only then does anything get sent or saved.

  • Scheduled routines
  • Draft, review, approve
  • Works across your mail, calendar, CRM and ERP

After go-live

What "in daily use" looks like once it is running

A working day from our own business, where TrueMake's agents run against live records. The agent prepares. A person approves. Nothing leaves without a signature.

  1. 07:30Morning brief lands: today's meetings, what came in overnight, the three tasks that slipped yesterdayagent
  2. 09:05Client call ends. Notes become tasks, time entries and a project note in the tracker, as draftsagent
  3. 09:20Reads the drafts, fixes one estimate, approves the restperson
  4. 11:40Inbox triaged. Two replies drafted with the right customer record pulled in from the CRMagent
  5. 11:50Edits one reply, sends bothperson
  6. 16:45Day's hours booked to projects. Friday's invoice drafted from the booked timeagent
  7. 17:00Signs off the invoiceperson
Every write goes through a connector that enforces the business's rules on the server.Audit trail on each change.

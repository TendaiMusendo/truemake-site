Our standard

Four things we always do

These are not a process diagram. They are the rules we run our own business by, and the reason a TrueMake engagement ends with something in use rather than something in a slide.

Criteria before building

Every phase starts with a short document you sign: each outcome, how it is measured, and the evidence that closes it. "Done" is never an opinion.

Scope stays fixed. Surprises stay small.

Sandbox before production

Connectors, flows and fields are created where they can be inspected and rolled back, and run end to end on real records before anything touches live data.

A failure in the sandbox costs an afternoon.

A person approves every send

Agents draft. People decide. Emails, invoices and record changes wait for a human signature, and the rule lives in the connector, not the prompt.

Trust is built one approved draft at a time.

Handover you can run without us

Code, connectors and a runbook written for the person who maintains it next. Ongoing support is available, but never required.

You own it. We are not a dependency.
